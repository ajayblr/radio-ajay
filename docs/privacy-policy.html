Privacy Policy

RadioAjay  ·  Last updated: June 2025

RadioAjay ("we", "our", or "us") is a free radio streaming app developed by Radio Ajay. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide the service:

  • Approximate location (country) — used only to suggest local radio stations by default. We do not store or transmit your precise location.
  • Usage analytics — anonymous app usage data (screens viewed, stations played) collected via Google Firebase Analytics to help us improve the app.
  • Favourites & preferences — stored locally on your device only. We do not sync or upload your saved stations.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.

Third-Party Services

RadioAjay uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Radio Browser API — provides the directory of radio stations. Station search and playback requests are sent to their servers.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics.

Audio Streaming

When you play a station, your device connects directly to that station's streaming server. The station provider may log your IP address as part of normal server operation. We have no control over third-party station servers.

Data Retention

Firebase Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default, per Google's standard policy. No other data is retained by us.

Children's Privacy

RadioAjay is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
